Urban Air Quality Forecasting Using Traffic and Weather Data

Spring 2025

Python Time Series XGBoost LSTM ARIMA Tableau

Introduction

This capstone project investigates the impact of weather and traffic on air pollution in NYC, Chicago, and LA. Using five years of historical data, we model pollutant levels (PM2.5, PM10, NO2) and identify key environmental drivers using machine learning and deep learning techniques.

Data Collection & Preparation

  • Pollution Data: Hourly EPA records (2020–2024)
  • Weather Data: Open-Meteo API (temperature, wind, humidity, precipitation)
  • Traffic Data: NYC MTA bridge/tunnel crossings
  • Transformation: Interpolation, scaling, rolling averages, and cyclic encoding

Modeling Approach

  • Regression Models: XGBoost (best performance), Random Forest
  • Time-Series Forecasting: LSTM (deep learning), ARIMA (statistical baseline)

Evaluation & Results

  • XGBoost: RMSE ≈ 4.35, R² > 0.85 for key city-pollutant pairs
  • LSTM: RMSE ≈ 4.42 (PM2.5), outperforming ARIMA significantly
  • Traffic Impact: Strong predictor for PM2.5 in NYC
  • Top Features: Wind speed and temperature consistently impactful

Interactive Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard allows exploration of pollutant trends and model predictions by city and pollutant.

Conclusion & Future Work

The study validates ML/AI approaches in pollution forecasting. Future enhancements include expanding to real-time prediction, geospatial layers, and integration with health data for policy planning.
